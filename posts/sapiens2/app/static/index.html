About

  • Models: Sapiens2 1B for pose (308 keypoints), body-part segmentation (29 classes, dome29 palette), surface normals, pointmap depth. Each model loads on first use and is cached on the GPU between requests.
  • Hover: pose dots show keypoint name + score; seg regions show body-part class names; surface normals and pointmap are plain images.
  • Image samples: 3 RGB studio + 4 grayscale ThermEval + 6 FLIR iron-palette thermals (harder cases) + 2 Wikimedia CC thermals. Upload your own to test.
  • Video samples: short Pexels CC0 clips of single men (crunches, meditation, downward-dog, walking). Inference at 1 fps by default (~1.2 s/frame on the A5000).
  • Thermal note: Sapiens2 was trained on RGB human imagery (Shutterstock / RenderPeople). Passing thermal IR through the RGB normalisation is honestly out-of-distribution. Body outline → seg and gross posture → pose tend to survive; surface normals and absolute depth do not.
